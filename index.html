A short market read · India

What the public signals suggest.

There isn't a reliable public ranking that isolates school and college students' perfume purchases. We used retailer popularity, visible review activity and listed prices as practical signals—not as a student survey or a definitive national sales chart.

Important: We have not surveyed students. Retailer ratings and bestseller labels reflect those platforms only; promotional prices change. “Long lasting” is often brand language and individual wear varies, so VELORA makes no untested hours-long claim.
